Privacy policy

What information we collect when you shop with us, why we need it, and your rights.

Who we are

This website is run by Anjan Panta, a sole trader trading as Acpanta ("Acpanta", "we", "us"), of 42 Rangland Road, London SE18 1HL. We are responsible for your personal information under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018).

What we collect

  • Order details: your name, delivery address, email address, phone number and what you bought.
  • Payment: payments are handled by Stripe (Stripe’s privacy policy). We don't see or store your full card details.
  • Messages: anything you send us by email, WhatsApp or Instagram.

Why we use it

  • To process, deliver and support your order, including returns. We need this to fulfil our contract with you.
  • To keep records for tax and accounting, as the law requires.
  • To answer your questions and improve our shop, which is in our legitimate interests.

Who we share it with

We only share what's needed with companies that help us run the shop: our payment provider, delivery companies and our website host. We never sell your personal information.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for 6 years for tax purposes.

Your shopping bag and fonts

This website remembers what's in your bag using storage in your own browser. That information stays on your device and isn't sent to us. Our fonts load from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your device's IP address when a page loads.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it, delete it or stop using it. To do this, contact us using the details below. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
