Privacy policy

What information we collect when you shop with us, why we need it, and your rights.

Who we are

This website is run by Anjan Panta, a sole trader trading as Acpanta ("Acpanta", "we", "us"), of 42 Rangland Road, London SE18 7LF. We are responsible for your personal information under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018).

What we collect

  • Order details: your name, delivery address, email address, phone number and what you bought.
  • Payment: payments are handled by Stripe (Stripe’s privacy policy). We don't see or store your full card details.
  • Messages: anything you send us by email, WhatsApp or Instagram.
  • Reviews: if you write a review, your first name and initial, rating, the product and your review, plus the email you used at checkout. We use the email only to check your order, and never show it. Only your first name and initial, rating, product, review, date and (if you give them) your height and size appear on the site.
  • Drop alerts and waitlists: if you sign up, your email or WhatsApp number, so we can tell you about new drops. We only message you if you tick the box, and you can unsubscribe any time by replying STOP or emailing us.
  • Custom requests: what you send on the customisation form, including any picture, so we can quote and make your piece.

Why we use it

  • To process, deliver and support your order, including returns. We need this to fulfil our contract with you.
  • To keep records for tax and accounting, as the law requires.
  • To answer your questions and improve our shop, which is in our legitimate interests.

Who we share it with

We only share what's needed with companies that help us run the shop: our payment provider, delivery companies and our website host. We never sell your personal information.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for 6 years for tax purposes. Drop alert and waitlist details are kept until you unsubscribe. Custom requests that don't become an order are deleted within 12 months. Published reviews stay up until you ask us to remove them. We delete the email sent with a review once we've checked it, and delete reviews we don't publish within 3 months.

Cookies

This website doesn't use cookies, analytics or advertising pixels, so there's no cookie banner. When you pay, Stripe's checkout page may use its own cookies to keep payments secure and prevent fraud; see Stripe's cookie policy. If we ever add analytics, we'll ask for your consent first.

Your shopping bag and fonts

This website remembers what's in your bag, the pieces you've saved or recently viewed, and the chest measurement you enter in Find my size, using storage in your own browser. That information stays on your device and isn't sent to us. You can clear it at any time by clearing your browser's site data. Our fonts load from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your device's IP address when a page loads.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it, delete it or stop using it. To do this, contact us using the details below. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
