Delivery

How much is delivery?
Nothing. Delivery is free on every order.
How long will my order take?
We aim to post it within 1–2 working days, and UK delivery usually takes 2–4 working days after that.
Do you deliver outside the UK?
Not yet. Checkout only accepts UK addresses for now.
How do I know it's been sent?
We'll email you when your order is posted.

Returns

Can I return something?
Yes. You've got 14 days from the day you receive your order to tell us you want to return it, for any reason. Then you have another 14 days to send it back.
How do I start a return?
Message us on WhatsApp or by email with your order number and what you're sending back. We'll confirm the address.
Who pays for return postage?
You do, unless the item is faulty or we sent the wrong thing. Use a tracked service, and send items back unworn and unwashed with the tags on.
When will I get my refund?
Within 14 days of us getting the item back, to the card you paid with.
Can I swap for a different size?
Return the item for a refund and place a new order for the size you want. That way you get it before it sells out.

Sizing

Which size should I get?
Check the size guide. When you pick a size on a product, it also shows the measurements and roughly which body chest it suits.
I'm between two sizes
Go down for a closer fit, or up if you like it roomy. The Boxy Hoodie is cut wide on purpose. Still unsure? Tell us your usual size on WhatsApp.
Will it shrink?
The Boxy Hoodie is pre-shrunk. For everything else, wash cool and skip the tumble dryer to keep the fit.

Orders & payment

How do I pay?
Payment goes through Stripe's secure checkout. We never see or store your card details.
Can I change or cancel my order?
Message us on WhatsApp as soon as you can. If it hasn't been posted yet, we'll sort it. If it has, you can return it.
Something's wrong with my order
Send us a photo on WhatsApp or by email. Faulty or wrong items can be returned for a full refund within 30 days, and we cover the postage.

Looking after your clothes

How should I wash them?
Wash inside out at 30°C with similar colours, and don't tumble dry. Turning them inside out protects the print. Always follow the care label too.
Can I iron the print?
Iron inside out and avoid ironing directly on the print.
