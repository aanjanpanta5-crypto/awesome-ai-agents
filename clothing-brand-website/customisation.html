Customisation

Make one of our tees or hoodies your own.

What we can do

  • Initials, a name or a date
  • Your own design or artwork
  • A choice of print position: front, back or sleeve

How it works

  1. Send us your idea on WhatsApp: which piece, colour and size, what you want on it, and a picture if you have one.
  2. We reply with a price and timing before you pay anything. Custom pieces take longer than our usual delivery.
  3. You approve it, pay, and we make it for you.

Good to know

  • Because a custom piece is made just for you, you can't return it because you've changed your mind. If it's faulty or not what we agreed, you're fully covered.
