Size guide

Lay a top you already like flat, measure it, and compare. Tap cm or in to switch.

Measurements

Chest is measured flat, pit to pit. Between two sizes? Go down for a closer fit, or up if you like it roomy. "Best for body chest" is measured around the fullest part of your chest.

Our fit: a relaxed, heavyweight streetwear fit with a structured, boxy drape.

  • Tees (260 GSM): true to size, with a clean everyday drape.
  • Hoodies and sweatshirts (320–430 GSM): premium boxy fit. Stay true to size for an oversized, boxy look, or size down for a standard fit.
  • Still unsure? Tell us your height and usual size on WhatsApp and we'll help you choose.

Exact measurements are on their way. We're measuring every size of the first batch so the numbers here are right.

Until then, message us and we'll send you the chest, length and sleeve for the size you want. If you're between two sizes, tell us your usual size and we'll help you choose.
