Our story

Made in small runs

Acpanta makes heavyweight basics and workwear in numbered batches of 300, with one family-run workshop and two mills.

How it's made

Four steps, one small workshop.

We work with a single family-run cut-and-sew house and two mills we've visited ourselves. Small runs mean less overstock, and we never mark down to clear a warehouse.

Every seam that takes strain is triple-needle stitched, so it holds up to years of hard wear.

  1. 1
    WeaveLoopwheel jersey and rope-dyed selvedge, woven slow on narrow looms.
  2. 2
    Cut300 pieces per run, hand-cut from paper patterns we revise each season.
  3. 3
    SewChain-stitched hems and flat-felled seams in dark red thread.
  4. 4
    Dye & washGarment-dyed after sewing, then pre-washed so the fit you buy is the fit you keep.
