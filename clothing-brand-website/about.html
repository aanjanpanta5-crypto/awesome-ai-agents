Our story Built for the present. Formed for everything.

Clothes that give you confidence.

I'm Anjan. I'm from Nepal, and my journey took me to Portugal and then to London. I started Acpanta because I wanted heavy, quality clothes: tees and hoodies that feel solid the moment you pick them up.

“Anything is possible.”

Anjan, founder of Acpanta

Every time I moved to a new country, I started again: a new place, new work, new people. When I wore clothes that were made well and felt good on me, I walked in with more confidence and more motivation.

For me, clothes are one of the most important parts of life. They speak before you do. What you wear makes you unique, and it's how people come to know you.

That's why every Acpanta piece is heavy, well made and kept simple. Wear it on your first day, on your next move, on your next big thing.

How it works

Not much stock, on purpose.

There are six pieces, in black or white. Every one is cut from premium-weight fabric. Most have the logo on.

I order small batches instead of filling a warehouse. So things sell out. When they do, they might come back different, or not at all.
