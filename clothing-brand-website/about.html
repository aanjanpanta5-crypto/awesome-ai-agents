Our story

Heavyweight, small batch

I'm Anjan. I started Acpanta in London because I wanted tees and hoodies with some weight to them. The kind that feel solid when you pick them up.

How it works

Not much stock, on purpose.

There are six pieces, in black or white. The lightest is a 260 GSM tee and the heaviest is a 430 GSM hoodie. Most have the logo on.

I order small batches instead of filling a warehouse. So things sell out. When they do, they might come back different, or not at all.
