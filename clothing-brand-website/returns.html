Delivery & returns

Every Acpanta piece is packed and posted by hand in London. Delivery to England, Scotland and Wales is £3.99, or free over £35, and if the fit isn't right, you have 14 days to send it back.

1. Delivery

Delivery in Great Britain: £3.99, free on orders over £35

  • Dispatch: orders are packed and dispatched within 1–2 working days of purchase.
  • Delivery: tracked with Royal Mail or Evri, taking 2–4 working days from dispatch.
  • Where we deliver: Great Britain only (England, Scotland and Wales). We can't deliver to Northern Ireland, the Channel Islands or the Isle of Man yet. If an order is placed to one of those addresses, we'll refund it in full.
  • Tracking: you'll get a tracking link by email as soon as your parcel leaves us in London.

Lost or damaged in the post

Your order is our responsibility until it's delivered to you. If tracking shows it hasn't arrived, or it arrives damaged, tell us and we'll send a replacement or refund you in full.

Changed your mind before it's posted?

Message us as soon as you can. If we haven't posted it yet, we'll cancel the order and refund everything, including delivery. If it's already on its way, you can still return it as below.

International shipping

We currently ship within Great Britain only. For international orders, message us on WhatsApp or email us before ordering.

2. Returns & exchanges

Under the UK Consumer Contracts Regulations, you have 14 calendar days from the day you receive your order to cancel it for a full refund or a size exchange. You don't need to give a reason.

The two deadlines, in plain words: tell us within 14 days of delivery that you want to return something, then post it back within 14 days of telling us.

When you cancel your whole order, we also refund the standard delivery charge you paid. Return postage is yours to pay unless the item is faulty or wrong.

Please send items back

  • Unworn, unwashed, and free from marks, scents or pet hair.
  • With the original tags and packaging still attached.
  • Within 14 days of telling us you want to return them.

If an item comes back worn, washed or damaged, we may reduce your refund, but only by the loss in value caused by handling it more than you'd need to check it (as you would in a shop). Trying it on to check the size is fine.

How to start a return

  1. Get in touch. Email aanjan.panta5@gmail.com or message us on WhatsApp at +44 7448 795690 within 14 days of delivery, with your order number and whether you'd like a refund or a different size.
  2. Pack and post. Send the items, with your order number inside, to the return address below. Return postage is paid by you unless the item is faulty or wrong. We recommend a tracked service, as returns are your responsibility until they reach us.

Size exchanges

Want a different size? Tell us when you get in touch. If your size is in stock, we'll send it once your return arrives. Every size of a piece costs the same, so there's nothing extra to pay. If it has sold out, we'll refund you instead.

Refunds

Once your parcel arrives back and we've checked it, we'll refund your original payment method within 3–5 working days. By law it will always be within 14 days of us receiving the items, or of you showing us proof that you've sent them, whichever is sooner. Your bank may take a few more days to show it.

Custom pieces

A custom piece is one we make or print to your own design, text or choices that you agreed with us before we made it (see Customisation). Our standard pieces in the shop are not custom, even in your chosen size and colour. Custom pieces can't be returned because you've changed your mind, as they're made just for you. If one is faulty or isn't what we agreed, the next section applies in full.

3. Faulty or incorrect items

If something arrives damaged, faulty or isn't what you ordered, contact us with your order number and a photo. Within 30 days of delivery you can return it for a full refund, or we'll send a replacement straight away if you prefer. After 30 days, we'll repair or replace it. If that isn't possible, or the repair or replacement doesn't fix the problem, you can ask for a price reduction or a refund. We pay any refund within 14 days of agreeing it, and we cover the return postage. This doesn't affect your legal rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015.
