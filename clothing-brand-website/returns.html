Returns & shipping

Free shipping on every order, and 14 days to change your mind.

Shipping

Delivery is free on every order. We aim to dispatch within [1–2] working days and delivery usually takes [2–4] working days within the UK. We'll email you when your order is on its way.

Returns: 14 days to change your mind

You can cancel your order for any reason within 14 days of the day you receive it. After you tell us, you then have another 14 days to send the items back.

  1. Tell us. Email or WhatsApp us with your order number and the items you're returning. You don't need to give a reason.
  2. Send it back. Post the items to the address we give you. Please return them unworn and unwashed, with the tags still attached.
  3. Get your refund. We refund you within 14 days of receiving the items back, or of you showing us proof that you've sent them, whichever is sooner. We refund to your original payment method.

You pay the cost of return postage, unless the item is faulty or we sent you the wrong item. We recommend a tracked service, as returns are your responsibility until they reach us. If an item comes back worn, washed or damaged, we may reduce your refund to reflect the loss in value.

Faulty or wrong items

If something arrives faulty, damaged or isn't what you ordered, contact us with a photo. Within 30 days of delivery you can return it for a full refund. After that, we'll repair or replace it, or refund you if that isn't possible. We cover the return postage. This doesn't affect your legal rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015.

Exchanges

Need a different size? Return the item for a refund and place a new order, so you get the size you want before it sells out.
